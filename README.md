# Didgeridoo 1.0

A didgeridoo you can play on your keyboard, mouse or touchscreen, for Windows and any modern browser. Hold to blow the drone, change the shape of your mouth to bring out the overtones, and add toots, barks, howls and tongue accents. Every sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files and the app needs no internet.

**▶ Play it in your browser: https://rpg86x.github.io/didgeridoo/**

![Didgeridoo](logo.png)

The didgeridoo (yidaki) comes from the Aboriginal peoples of northern Australia, who have played it for well over a thousand years. This app is a simple tribute to that sound. The painted bands and dots are plain decoration and do not copy any traditional design.

## Didgeridoos

| # | Key | Length | Character |
|---|-----|--------|-----------|
| 1 | B1 | ≈ 157 cm | Very deep and slow, for stillness and grounding |
| 2 | C2 | ≈ 148 cm | Dark and full, a calm drone for meditation |
| 3 | D2 | ≈ 132 cm | The all-rounder most players start with |
| 4 | E2 | ≈ 118 cm | Lively and clear, good for rhythm |
| 5 | F2 | ≈ 110 cm | Bright and quick, easy to play fast |
| 6 | G2 | ≈ 98 cm | Short and high, for sharp rhythms |

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `Space` (hold) or press and hold the didgeridoo | Blow the drone |
| `A` `S` `D` `F` `G` (hold) | Blow with a mouth shape: oo, oh, ah, eh, ee |
| Slide over the mouth strip or the didgeridoo | Change the mouth shape smoothly |
| `Z` (hold) | Toot: the overblown trumpet note |
| `X` / `C` | Bark / howl through the tube |
| `V` (hold) | Growl: your voice on top of the drone |
| `J` / `K` | Accents: a hard "ta" / a soft "wah" |
| `W` | Keep droning, with slow mouth movement and breath pulses |
| `R` | Rhythm (set the speed with the Tempo slider) |
| `Esc` or `Backspace` | Silence |
| `<` `>` / `,` `.` / `←` `→` / `1`–`6` | Switch didgeridoo |

Volume, reverb and tempo sliders are at the bottom of the window. The app remembers your settings.

## Languages

English (default) and Dutch, plus German, French and Spanish. Pick another language in the menu at the top right. On first start the app uses your system language if it's available.

## Install (Windows)

1. Download this repository (**Code → Download ZIP**) and unzip it.
2. Double-click `launch.vbs`. The app opens in its own Microsoft Edge window.

You can also open `index.html` in any modern browser, including on a phone or tablet.
